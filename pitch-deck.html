Pitch Deck • First Draft

KaziLink

Connecting young talent to real opportunities.

Keach Jock Chuol
Founder • Concept stage

02 • Problem

Talent exists. Opportunity is fragmented.

  • Entry-level opportunities are scattered across social media, groups and job sites.
  • Young people struggle to tell which opportunities are trustworthy.
  • Employers spend time searching for suitable junior talent.
  • Students often lack practical mentors and career-readiness support.
03 • Solution

One trusted pathway from learning to opportunity.

Verified opportunities

Jobs, internships, fellowships and projects.

Talent profiles

Skills, projects, education and interests.

Mentorship

Practical guidance from professionals.

Employer matching

Discover candidates by skills and fit.

04 • Why now

The opportunity is becoming digital.

  • Hiring and career discovery increasingly happen through digital channels.
  • Students already use mobile platforms to learn, network and search.
  • Remote and project-based work creates new entry points.
  • Employers need efficient access to emerging talent.
05 • Customer

Start with the people who need the bridge most.

Primary users

Students • recent graduates • early-career professionals • self-taught learners

Paying customers

Startups • SMEs • NGOs • training providers • junior-talent recruiters

Initial focus: East African emerging talent and organizations.

07 • Business model

Free for talent; paid for high-value access.

Talent

Free profile and opportunity access; optional premium career tools.

Employers

Paid listings, candidate search and matching fees.

Partners

Sponsored programs and institutional partnerships.

Revenue

Subscriptions • featured listings • matching • premium services • sponsorships

08 • Go-to-market

Build trust through communities first.

Phase 1

Universities, student groups, ambassadors and manually verified opportunities.

Phase 2

Recruit startups, SMEs and organizations with recurring junior hiring needs.

Phase 3

Referrals, campus networks, employer partnerships and geographic expansion.

North star

Users return because relevant opportunities are consistently trustworthy.
