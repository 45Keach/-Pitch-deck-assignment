## Slide 1 — Cover
**KaziLink**
### Connecting young talent to real opportunities
A digital platform helping students and early-career professionals discover verified jobs, internships, mentorship and practical career opportunities.

**Founder:** Keach Jock Chuol  
**Stage:** First draft / concept

---

## Slide 2 — The Problem
### Talent exists. Opportunity is fragmented.

- Young people struggle to find trustworthy entry-level opportunities in one place.
- Job and internship information is scattered across social media, messaging groups and multiple websites.
- Many opportunities are difficult to verify, creating uncertainty and wasted time.
- Employers and organizations spend time searching for suitable junior talent.
- Students often lack access to mentors who can help them move from learning to employment.

**Core problem:** The transition from education to meaningful work is harder than it needs to be.

---

## Slide 3 — The Solution
### One trusted pathway from learning to opportunity.

**KaziLink** brings together:
1. **Verified opportunities** — internships, entry-level jobs, fellowships and projects.
2. **Talent profiles** — skills, projects, education and interests in one place.
3. **Mentorship** — practical guidance from professionals.
4. **Career resources** — CV, interview and workplace-readiness tools.
5. **Employer matching** — organizations can discover candidates based on skills and fit.

**Simple promise:** Find opportunities. Build skills. Meet mentors. Get started.

---

## Slide 4 — Why Now
### The opportunity is becoming digital.

- Employers increasingly use digital channels to identify and screen talent.
- Students and young professionals already use mobile platforms to learn, network and search for work.
- Remote and project-based work creates new pathways into the workforce.
- Organizations need more efficient ways to reach emerging talent.
- A mobile-first, trust-focused platform can reduce friction for both sides.

**Timing:** The infrastructure and behavior are ready for a simpler career-access platform.

---

**Primary users**

- Recent graduates

**Paying customers**
- Startups and SMEs

## Slide 8 — Go-to-Market
### Build trust through communities first.

**Phase 1 — Community**
- Partner with universities, student groups and training programs.
- Recruit early users through ambassadors.
- Curate verified opportunities manually.

**Phase 2 — Employers**
- Approach startups, SMEs and organizations with recurring junior hiring needs.
- Offer early employer partners simple, low-friction listings.
- Measure applications and successful matches.

**Phase 3 — Scale**
- Referral loops
- Campus ambassador network
- Employer partnerships
- Content and career education
- Expansion into additional cities and markets

**North-star behavior:** A user returns because KaziLink consistently surfaces relevant, trustworthy opportunities.

---

## Slide 9 — Competition
### Users already have alternatives — but the experience is fragmented.

| Alternative | Strength | Gap KaziLink addresses |
|---|---|---|
| General job boards | Large listings | Can be broad and noisy for entry-level users |
| Social media groups | Fast community sharing | Verification and organization can be inconsistent |
| University career offices | Trusted local support | Limited reach and opportunity volume |
| Professional networks | Strong profiles/networking | Can be difficult for first-time job seekers |
| KaziLink | Focused career pathway | Builds around verified entry-level opportunities + mentorship |

**Differentiation:** Trust + youth focus + mentorship + opportunity matching in one workflow.

## Slide 10 — Traction & Validation Plan
### Prove the problem before scaling the product.

**Initial validation goals**
- Interview 30–50 students/recent graduates.
- Interview 10–15 employers or organizations.
- Build a waitlist of 100+ target users.
- Secure 10+ pilot employers/partners.
- Manually curate the first 50–100 verified opportunities.

**Metrics to track**
- Weekly active users
- Applications per opportunity
- Profile completion
- Employer repeat posting
- Interview-to-application conversion
- Successful placements
- User referral rate

**Current stage:** Concept / first validation draft.  
No traction is claimed yet.
